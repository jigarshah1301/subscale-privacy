1. Data Collection (Data Safety Compliance)

To provide app functionality and maintain reliability, we collect the following information.

Device Identifiers

Anonymous device identifiers generated by Google Firebase may be used for app functionality, crash reporting, and fraud prevention.

App Performance

We collect crash logs and diagnostic data via Google Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance Tools to monitor application performance and ensure stability.

Personal Information (Optional)

Users may optionally provide an email address and/or mobile number for personalization purposes. Providing this information is completely voluntary, and the app functions without it. If provided, this information is securely linked to your unique identifier to personalize your experience.

2. Account Identity & Subscription Records

SubScale is designed to be used immediately without requiring you to use an external account (such as a Google or Apple ID) or create your own account.

Subscription service names, categories, renewal dates, renewal frequency, payment methods (optional), costs and user-generated notes (optional) that the Users manually enter in the App are stored in a secure Google Cloud Firestore database and are linked only to an anonymous Firebase user identifier (UID).

Some subscription information may also be temporarily stored locally on your device to enable offline access and faster performance.

3. Data Handling & Security

Encryption

All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/SSL protocols.

Zero Sharing

We do not share or sell any data to third-party marketers or brokers.

No Ads

The app is 100% advertisement-free.

4. App Permissions

Device Permissions (Local Notifications)

To provide timely "Renewal Alerts" for your subscriptions, SubScale utilizes local device notifications. These alerts are scheduled and generated entirely on your device; we do not use external push notification servers to deliver them.

We will request your explicit permission before enabling alerts, and you can modify your preferences or disable them completely at any time within the app settings or your device's system settings.

5. Data Deletion (Your Rights)

Users have the right to delete their data. We provide a Self-Service Data Deletion tool within the app settings that allows you to:

  • Wipe all local app data
  • Permanently delete all Cloud Firestore records and your anonymous Firebase UID

6. Children's Privacy

SubScale is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not collect personal information from children.

7. Transparency Commitment

SubScale is designed with a privacy-first philosophy. We intentionally collect the minimum amount of data necessary to operate the application reliably.

We do not build advertising profiles, we do not sell user data, and we do not track users across other apps or websites.

SubScale is an informational tool designed to help users track subscriptions and recurring expenses. It does not provide financial, investment, or payment services.

SubScale does not connect to Bank Accounts, Credit Cards, Payment Gateways or Financial Institutions. All subscription details are manually entered by the Users.

8. Independent Tool Status

SubScale is an independent subscription management tool. We use company logos and trademarks solely for the purpose of identifying subscription services for users.

SubScale is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of the trademark owners whose logos appear within the app. All trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.

9. Third-Party Services

SubScale may use trusted third-party services to support core app functionality and reliability. These services may process limited technical data necessary for their operation.

SubScale currently uses Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Authentication (Anonymous UID)
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore (Secure Data Storage)
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Crash Reporting)
  • Firebase Performance Tool (Performance Monitoring)

We utilize Currency APIs — Frankfurter API and ExchangeRate-API for real time currency conversions.

iTunes Search API (App Store Service) and Google S2 Favicon API are used to fetch Brand Logos.

When these services are used, your device may temporarily communicate with their servers, which may result in the transmission of your IP address strictly for the purpose of fulfilling the requested functionality.
