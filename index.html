SubScale: Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 11, 2026

1. Independent Tool Status

SubScale is an independent subscription management tool. We use company logos and trademarks for the sole purpose of identifying subscription services for the user. SubScale is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of the trademark owners whose logos appear within the app. All trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.

2. Data Collection (Data Safety Compliance)

To provide app functionality and maintain reliability, we collect:

  • Personal Information (Optional): You may optionally provide an Email Address or Mobile Number. This is used solely for personalisation and is 100% voluntary.
  • Device Identifiers: We collect Device IDs (via Firebase) for App Functionality, Analytics, and Fraud Prevention.
  • App Performance: We collect Crash Logs and Diagnostics (via Firebase Crashlytics) to ensure stability.
  • Subscription Records: Your service names and costs are stored in a secure Firestore database siloed to your Anonymous UID.

3. Data Handling & Security

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/SSL protocols.
  • Zero Sharing: We do not share or sell any data to third-party marketers or brokers.
  • No Ads: The app is 100% advertisement-free.

4. Data Deletion (Your Rights)

Users have the right to be forgotten. We provide a Self-Service Data Deletion tool within the app settings that allows you to:

  • Wipe all local app data.
  • Permanently delete all Cloud Firestore records and your Anonymous UID.
