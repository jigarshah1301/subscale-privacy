SubScale Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SubScale handles information when you use the SubScale mobile application. By using the application, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy. This policy applies only to the SubScale application and its related services.

1. Data Collection (Data Safety Compliance)

To provide app functionality and maintain reliability, we collect the following information:

Personal Information (Optional)

Users may optionally provide an email address and/or mobile number for personalization purposes. Providing this information is completely voluntary, and the app functions without it.

Device Identifiers

Anonymous device identifiers generated by Google Firebase may be used for app functionality, analytics, crash reporting, and fraud prevention.

App Performance

We collect crash logs and diagnostic data via Google Firebase Crashlytics to monitor application performance and ensure stability.

Subscription Records

Your subscription service names and costs are stored in a secure Google Cloud Firestore database and are linked only to an anonymous Firebase user identifier (UID).

2. Data Handling & Security

Encryption

All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/SSL protocols.

Zero Sharing

We do not share or sell any data to third-party marketers or brokers.

No Ads

The app is 100% advertisement-free.

3. Data Deletion (Your Rights)

Users have the right to delete their data. We provide a Self-Service Data Deletion tool within the app settings that allows you to:

Wipe all local app data & permanently delete all Cloud Firestore records and your anonymous Firebase UID

4. Children's Privacy

SubScale is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not collect personal information from children.

5. Transparency Commitment

SubScale is designed with a privacy-first philosophy. We intentionally collect the minimum amount of data necessary to operate the application reliably.

We do not build advertising profiles, we do not sell user data, and we do not track users across other apps or websites.

6. Independent Tool Status

SubScale is an independent subscription management tool. We use company logos and trademarks solely for the purpose of identifying subscription services for users. SubScale is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of the trademark owners whose logos appear within the app. All trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.

7. Third-Party Services

SubScale may use trusted third-party services to support core app functionality and reliability.

These services may process limited technical data necessary for their operation. SubScale currently uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Analytics, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Crashlytics to provide app functionality, analytics, and crash reporting.

These services operate under their own privacy policies. SubScale does not sell or share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.
